# Hi, I'm Lei Li 👋

[Assistant Professor of Data Science](https://datascience.virginia.edu/people/lei-li) at the University of Virginia, leading the **Spatial AI Lab (SAIL)**. My research interests are in computer vision and computer graphics, with a focus on advancing machine spatial intelligence to perceive, understand, and interact with the 3D world.

## 📄 Selected Papers

- [GenZI](https://craigleili.github.io/projects/genzi/): Zero-Shot 3D Human-Scene Interaction Generation. *CVPR 2024*
- [HOI-PAGE](https://craigleili.github.io/projects/hoipage/): Zero-Shot Human-Object Interaction Generation with Part Affordance Guidance. *ICML 2026*
- [Seen2Scene](https://quan-meng.github.io/projects/seen2scene/): Completing Realistic 3D Scenes with Visibility-Guided Flow. *ECCV 2026*
- [MeshPad](https://derkleineli.github.io/meshpad/): Interactive Sketch-Conditioned Artist-Reminiscent Mesh Generation and Editing. *ICCV 2025*
- [MeshArt](https://daoyig.github.io/Mesh_Art/): Generating Articulated Meshes with Structure-Guided Transformers. *CVPR 2025*
